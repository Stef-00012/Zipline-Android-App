Zipline is an app that allows you to manage the files & URLs and shorten URLs & upload files to your self-hosted Zipline instance (Requires Zipline v4.2.3 or greater).

Upload Files:
You can upload any file to your Zipline instance along with all of its settings such as hide the file behind a password, limit the amount of views it can receive or delete it after a specified amount of time.

Manage Files:
You can see, download, edit and delete all the files on your Zipline instance.

Shorten URLs:
You can shorten any URL through your Zipline instance with an optional vanity ID, password or max views.

Manage URLs:
You can open, edit and delete all the shortened URLs on your Zipline instance.

Manage Folders:
You can create, edit, delete, view and upload files to the folders you own on your Zipline instance.

View Metrics:
You can view the stats of your uploads such as storage used, views, total files and URLs.

Manage your own User:
You can change your username, password, avatar and any other personal setting (except for OAuth and sessions).

Manage other Users:
If you are an admin, you can create, edit, delete and view the files of any user on your Zipline instance.

Manage Settings:
If you are a Superadmin, you can view and modify the settings of your Zipline instance such as maximum file size, rateilimits etc.

The app is open source and available on GitHub: https://git.stefdp.com/Stef/Zipline-Android-App.
