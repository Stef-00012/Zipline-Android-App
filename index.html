Zipline Android

Zipline is an Android app to manage your self-hosted Zipline instance.

Features

Upload Files. You can upload any file to your Zipline instance along with all of its settings such as hide the file behind a password, limit the amount of views it can receive or delete it after a specified amount of time.

Manage Files. You can see, download, edit and delete all the files on your Zipline instance.

Shorten URLs. You can shorten any URL through your Zipline instance with an optional vanity ID, password or max views.

Manage URLs. You can open, edit and delete all the shortened URLs on your Zipline instance.

Manage Folders. You can create, edit, delete, view and upload files to the folders you own on your Zipline instance.

View Metrics. You can view the stats of your uploads such as storage used, views, total files and URLs.

Manage your own User. You can change your username, password, avatar and any other personal setting (except for OAuth and sessions).

Manage other Users. If you are an admin, you can create, edit, delete and view the files of any user on your Zipline instance.

Manage Settings. If you are a Superadmin, you can view and modify the settings of your Zipline instance such as maximum file size, rateilimits etc.

Share to Zipline. Share files & URLs to the app to upload/shorten them on your Zipline instance.

Background Uploads. Allows uploading files in the background with a notification showing the process (might not work correctly if the notifications permission is denied).
